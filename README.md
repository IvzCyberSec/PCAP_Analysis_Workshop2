# PCAP_Analysis_Workshop2
In this repo you will go through the analysis of **PCAP (Packet Capture) files** 
And write a report about your findings. PCAP analysis is very important in Cyber Security (Or any other networking role)
PCAP analysis is a cornerstone of cybersecurity as it provides an unalterable, line BY line record of exactly what happened during a cyberattack.

## What you will learn

- Open and navigate PCAP files.
- Analyse network traffic.
- Identify protocols, IP addresses and ports.
- Follow network conversations.
- Use filters to investigate specific traffic.
- Identify suspicious or unusual activity.
- Extract evidence from packets.
- Write a short technical report based on your findings.

## Tool
### Wireshark

You will use **Wireshark** for the majority of the analysis.
Wireshark allows you to capture and inspect network traffic 
packet-by-packet and provides useful filtering.
Key features to explore:

- Packet list and packet details
- Display filters
- Protocol analysis
- Follow TCP streams!
- Conversations
- Endpoints
- Statistics
- Exporting relevant evidence
- CREDENTIALS!

## Workshop Task

You will be provided with one or more PCAP files.
Your task is to investigate the traffic and determine:

1. What systems are communicating?
2. Which protocols and ports are being used?
3. What network activity took place?
4. Is there anything unusual or suspicious?
5. What evidence supports your findings?

Record and report on any finding.

## Report

Your final report should briefly include:

- **Introduction** - what PCAP was analysed.
- **Methodology** - how you investigated the traffic.
- **Findings** - important traffic, hosts, protocols and evidence.
- **Analysis** - what the findings mean.
- **Conclusion** - summary of what you discovered.

Include relevant screenshots or packet information where appropriate.

## Important

Focus on **evidence from the PCAP** rather than assumptions.
Your goal is to investigate the traffic and explain what happened based on the evidence available to you.
